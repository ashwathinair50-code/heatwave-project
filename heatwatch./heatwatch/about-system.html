About the Heatwave Intelligence System

What the system is for, where its data comes from and who uses it.

Section colours follow the severity scale used across the portal: Low Moderate High Extreme

Objective

The system collects temperature and humidity readings, classifies the heat risk for each location and helps officials issue early warnings before conditions become dangerous. Risk is judged from the heat index, written as HImax in bulletins, and the final decision always stays with a trained officer.

Data Sources

Readings arrive from automatic weather stations, a national weather database and satellite feeds. Citizens can also submit observations through the observation form. Details are listed on the sources page.

Stakeholders

Meteorologists analyse readings, disaster-management officers plan the response, and citizens receive advice. Each role and its actions are listed on the sources page.
