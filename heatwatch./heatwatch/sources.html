SOURCES & ROLES

Data sources & stakeholders

How a reading becomes an alert, where data comes from and who does what.

Forecast and alert workflow

  1. Collect readings from weather stations.
  2. Check and clean the data.
  3. Compare readings with the heatwave thresholds.
  4. Classify the severity level.
  5. Prepare and approve the alert bulletin.
  6. Send the alert to subscribers and agencies.
  7. Review conditions and revise the bulletin.

Data sources

  • AWS sensors (temperature and humidity)
  • IMD database (historical and forecast data)
  • Satellite feeds (land-surface temperature)
  • Citizen observations submitted through the observation form

Stakeholders and their actions

  • Citizens
    • Subscribe to advisories
    • Read safety guidance
    • Submit local observations
  • Meteorologists
    • Analyse station and satellite data
    • Classify severity
    • Publish and revise bulletins
  • Disaster-management officers
    • Activate response plans
    • Open cooling centres
    • Coordinate with hospitals and local bodies
  • Health workers
    • Prepare for heat-related cases
    • Advise vulnerable groups

Abbreviations

AWS
Automatic Weather Station: an unattended station that records temperature, humidity and other weather values.
IMD
India Meteorological Department: the national agency for weather observation and forecasting.
HW
Heatwave: a spell of abnormally high temperature that can harm health.
SHW
Severe Heatwave: a heatwave that crosses the higher severity threshold and needs stronger action.
