Severe Heatwave Alert Bulletin

A worked example of how a bulletin is written and formatted.

Severe Heatwave Alert for Vidarbha Region

Issued on 15th May 2026 · Bulletin version V2.1

The AWS network reports a maximum temperature of 47°C at Nagpur and neighbouring stations. This bulletin is modelled on the style of IMD heatwave communications and classifies the situation as a SHW, one level above an ordinary HW.

Advisory themes

  • Stay hydrated: drink water (H2O) or oral rehydration solution regularly.
  • Avoid peak hours: postpone outdoor work between 12:00 and 16:00.
  • Protect the vulnerable: check on older adults, children and outdoor workers.

Peak heat index (HImax) is expected to remain above 50°C for three consecutive days.

Forecast subject to revision. This is a sample bulletin for learning purposes and is not an official warning.
