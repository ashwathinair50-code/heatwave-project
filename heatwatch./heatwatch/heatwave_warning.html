Heatwave risk and early-warning decision

Recommends a warning level from temperature, humidity and the forecast. Change the values and press Assess.

Decision rules used (in this order)
    

  • Temperature ≥ 40°C: Severe Heatwave Warning
    • 

  • Temperature ≥ 38°C and humidity ≥ 60%: High Heat Risk, issue warning
    • 

  • Forecast ≥ 40°C: Early Warning, prepare for heatwave
    • 

  • Temperature 35°C up to 40°C: Moderate Heat Risk, monitor conditions
    • 

  • Below 35°C: Normal, continue monitoring

The sheet’s table stops the moderate band at 38°C, which leaves 38–40°C with low humidity and a mild forecast without any answer. This page closes that gap by extending Moderate to 40°C.
